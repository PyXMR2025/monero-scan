**MK7 venues:**
### **🇵🇱 Kinoteka, Warsaw**
This is the same venue as was used for MK6. It's a cinema located on the first few floors of the Palace of Science and Culture in the very heart of Warsaw. **Picking this venue allows us to collaborate again with the Bitcoin Film Fest** and the Revolution.rocks music festival, although per the feedback we received we would either take a different floor or different dates to give us some extra space.

📅 Dates:
If the community picks this venue we'll do another meeting at a different date to decide the dates.

https://drive.proton.me/urls/5AC1Y0EBDR#3dbZnbGVVTh3

### **🇵🇱 Forum + ICE, Krakow**
Forum is a collection of bars, a cafe, multiple small restaurants (Mexican, Italian, Thai, Greek, sushi). They also have arcades, pool tables, bowling and shuffleboards. They have space for booths, a workshop area, a basic main stage and a river-front terrace. 

All attractions, restaurants and bars within Forum can be paid for using magnetic cards. This potentially allows us to sell top ups for XMR which would allow attendees to pay for everything with XMR.

ICE is a congress centre. It's boring, but it has good stages. We would use it for longer content (ex: talks longer than 20min, panels, etc), most likely by taking the S3 Chamber Hall.

The venues are 6 min away by foot conveniently connected by an underground pedestrian crossing.
There's a direct bus from the airport to these venues that leaves as often as every 10 min.

📅 Dates:
9-11 July 2027
ICE has no earlier suitable dates available.

### **🇨🇿 Pragovka, Prague**
It's a freshly renovated warehouse. 
  
- A bit tight, but manageable. It has just enough space for sponsor booths and workshops.
- Features a nearby café serving alcohol, AC, and an acceptable (though barely green) outdoor area.
- No interior CCTV. Located directly across the road from a shooting range.
- Near the yellow (B) metro line, but still a 30-minute commute from the city center.

📅 Dates:
Any, except June 8-9. If the community picks this venue we'll do another meeting at a different date to decide the dates.
