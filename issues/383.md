# docs: add transaction finality section to technical specs
author: Flotapponnier
created_at: 2026-08-12T01:13:58Z
status: closed

## Summary

Adds a **Transaction finality** section to `technical-specs.md`, right after the Block time section.

The section explains Monero's probabilistic finality model and references the 10-confirmation threshold already cited in the [`--reorg-notify` monerod option](https://monerodocs.org/interacting/monerod-reference/#options):

> "Simply account for reorganizations by requiring at least 10 confirmations before shipping valuable goods."

It also links to [OpenChainBench L1 Finality](https://openchainbench.com/benchmarks/l1-finality) as a live cross-chain reference, so developers can see Monero's ~20 min settlement time in context alongside Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and others.
